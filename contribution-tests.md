# Contribution timestamp tests

These calendar samples were generated on November 16, 2026. Their author and
committer dates are intentionally backdated and do not represent work
performed on those dates.

The sample range is November 16, 2022 through November 16, 2026, inclusive.
Saturdays, Sundays, Republic Day (January 26), Independence Day (August 15),
and Gandhi Jayanti (October 2) are excluded. Original project commits are
preserved unchanged.

Randomly selected dates were subsequently removed from this sample history.
